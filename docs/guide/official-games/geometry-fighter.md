# tinker-geometry-fighter

Survive escalating swarms in a twin-stick geometry shooter. A neon arcade game for [TINKER](https://github.com/liriliri/tinker).

![Screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/liriliri/tinker-games/master/packages/tinker-geometry-fighter/screenshot.png)

## Highlights

- **Twin-stick combat** — move with one stick, aim and fire with the other
- **Rising pressure** — waves get denser as you last, and kill chains raise the score multiplier
- **Field pickups** — a shield, a slow field, a screen-clearing bomb, plus drifting weapon and bomb capsules
- **Neon grid** — glowing vector ships, bloom, and a high score saved locally

## Installation

Play directly in [browser](https://tinker.liriliri.io/games/geometry-fighter/), or install via TINKER: download from `https://tinker.liriliri.io/`, then run `npm i -g tinker-geometry-fighter`.

## How to Play

1. Open the **Geometry Fighter** plugin in TINKER
2. **Click** or press fire to start
3. Move with **WASD** or the **left stick**. Aim with the **mouse** or the **right stick** — hold fire, or deflect the right stick, to shoot
4. Set off a **bomb** with the right mouse button or the right trigger
5. Grab drifting capsules for a wider shot or an extra bomb. Blinking items grant a shield, slow every enemy, or detonate the whole field
6. Press **Escape** or **Start** to pause. Lose all lives and it's game over — fire again to retry
