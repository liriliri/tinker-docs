# tinker-jump-jump

Hold to charge, release to jump — land on the next platform! A 3D skill game for [TINKER](https://github.com/liriliri/tinker), inspired by the classic WeChat Jump Jump.

![Screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/liriliri/tinker-games/master/packages/tinker-jump-jump/screenshot.png)

## Highlights

- **One-touch control** — hold to charge power, release to leap toward the next block
- **Perfect landings** — hit the center for combo bonuses and rising score multipliers
- **Polished 3D stage** — glass bottle character, varied platforms, soft shadows, and wave rings
- **Classic feel** — timing-based jumps with familiar SFX for charge, land, combo, and fall
- **Beat your best** — high score is saved locally so you can keep chasing records

## Installation

Play directly in [browser](https://tinker.liriliri.io/games/jump-jump/), or install via TINKER: download from `https://tinker.liriliri.io/`, then run `npm i -g tinker-jump-jump`.

## How to Play

1. Open the **Jump Jump** plugin in TINKER
2. **Hold** (mouse, touch, or Space) to charge — the longer you hold, the farther you jump
3. **Release** to leap toward the next platform
4. Land on the block to score; hit the **center** for a perfect combo
5. Miss the platform and it's game over — tap **Play Again** to retry
