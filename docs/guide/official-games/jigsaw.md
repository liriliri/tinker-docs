# tinker-jigsaw

Drag the pieces together until the picture is whole again! A parade-style jigsaw puzzle for [TINKER](https://github.com/liriliri/tinker).

![Screenshot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/liriliri/tinker-games/master/packages/tinker-jigsaw/screenshot.png)

## Highlights

- **Living gallery** — dozens of cinematic Chinese-style plates, from wuxia bridges to cloud temples
- **Parade of pieces** — cards drift along the edge; grab each one and snap it home before it slips away
- **Three difficulties** — every picture unlocks easy, medium, and hard grids that scale with the image itself
- **Bring your own** — pick a photo from disk and turn it into a fresh puzzle
- **Star progress** — clears are saved per picture so you can climb the ranks over time

## Installation

Play directly in [browser](https://tinker.liriliri.io/games/jigsaw/), or install via TINKER: download from `https://tinker.liriliri.io/`, then run `npm i -g tinker-jigsaw`.

## How to Play

1. Open the **Jigsaw** plugin in TINKER
2. Choose a picture from the gallery, or tap the blank slot to load your own
3. Drag pieces from the moving parade onto their place on the board
4. Chain snaps for combos — miss a piece and it gets away
5. Clear the picture, then try the next star difficulty
