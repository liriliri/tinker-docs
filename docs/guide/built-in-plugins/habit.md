# tinker-habit

A habit check-in tracker plugin for [TINKER](https://github.com/liriliri/tinker), with weekday, weekly, or monthly schedules and a month calendar view.

![Screenshot](/habit.png)

## Features

- **Flexible schedules** — set habits by specific weekdays, times per week, or times per month
- **Month calendar** — view check-ins on a monthly calendar with habit colors
- **Day sidebar** — select a date and toggle check-ins for habits due that day
- **Habits list** — manage all habits with completion rate, streak, and period progress
- **Custom colors** — assign a color to each habit for calendar and sidebar display
- **End or delete** — end a habit while keeping history, or delete it with all check-ins
- **Toolbar navigation** — browse months, jump to today, and show or hide the sidebar

## Usage

1. Click the **+** button in the toolbar to add a new habit
2. Enter a title, pick a color, and choose a schedule type (specific weekdays, times per week, or times per month)
3. Click a date on the calendar to select it; the Day sidebar lists habits due that day
4. Click a habit in the Day sidebar to check in or undo the check-in
5. Switch to the Habits tab to see completion rate, streak, and period progress for each habit
6. Edit, end, or delete a habit from the Habits list (or via right-click context menu)
7. Use the toolbar arrows to change months, **Today** to jump to the current date, and the panel button to toggle the sidebar
